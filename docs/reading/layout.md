# Zoom, layout and page turning

## Zoom any page

Pinch on the page, wherever it is open: alone, beside your notes, side by side with another book, or in Focus.

| Device | How |
| --- | --- |
| Phone or tablet | Spread two fingers apart to zoom in, pinch them together to zoom out |
| Laptop touchpad | The same, with two fingers on the touchpad |
| Mouse | Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> on a Mac) and turn the wheel |

What zooming does depends on what is open:

- **PDFs, documents, slides, pictures, DjVu:** the page grows or shrinks; scroll to see the parts that no longer fit.
- **E-books, text and Markdown files:** the words grow or shrink and the lines wrap again, as on an e-reader. It is the same as **Aa**, **Text size**.

Only the page zooms, never the buttons around it.

## Zoom a PDF with the buttons

- Click **−** or **+** in the book's bar.
- Click the percentage (**82%**) to fit the page to the width of the panel again.

When you make the panel wider or narrower, a page fitted to the width stays fitted.

## Fit the whole page, two pages, rotate (PDF)

1. Click **⋯ Tools** in the book's bar.
2. Under **Pages**, choose:
   - **Fit width** or **Whole page**
   - **Two pages**, like an open book (choose it again for **One page at a time**)
   - **Turn**, for pages printed sideways

## Let the page scroll by itself (PDF)

Good for reading a long document at a steady pace, hands free.

1. Click **⋯ Tools**.
2. Next to **Scroll by itself**, choose **Slow**, **Steady** or **Fast**.

A **Stop scrolling** button appears in the bar. Click it, or choose **Off**, to stop.

## Turn pages like paper

PDFs, e-books and slides can turn like a real book: drag or swipe a corner, or use the arrow keys.

1. Click **⋯ Tools**.
2. Click **Like paper**.

To go back, open **⋯ Tools** and click **Back to scrolling** (PDF) or **Like paper** again (e-books). Sidepage remembers your choice for next time.

## Text size, spacing and typeface (e-books)

In an EPUB or other e-book:

1. Click **Text size and typeface** in the book's bar (the **Aa** icon).
2. Click **Larger text** or **Smaller text**, **Looser lines** or **Tighter lines**, and pick a typeface.

For an e-book you can also choose **Read as one long scroll** in **⋯ Tools**, instead of page by page.

## Light, dark and sepia

See [Language, theme and paper](/settings/look). Dark themes darken the page too, while keeping photos and diagrams in their real colours.

## Panels

- **Make the book wider:** drag the line between the book and the notes.
- **Fill the window with the book:** click **⤢** at the end of the book's tab strip. Click it again to bring the notes back.
- **Notes under the book instead of beside it:** **More**, **Settings**, **Look**, **Layout**, then **Book above notes**.
