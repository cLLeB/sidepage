# Scanned pages and pictures

A scanned PDF, a photo of a page or a slide made of pictures has no words in it, only an image of words. Sidepage can read the words in such pictures (this is called OCR) so you can copy them, search them and study them.

Reading the words happens on your device. The first time, Sidepage fetches its reading engine from the internet; after that it works offline.

## Choose the language first

Reading is more accurate when Sidepage knows the language.

1. Open **More** (⋯ at the top right).
2. Under **Reading**, set **Text in pictures** to the language of your documents: English, French, English and French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian or Dutch.

## Make a whole scanned book searchable

When you open a scanned PDF, the book's bar shows **Scanned: make it searchable**.

1. Click **Scanned: make it searchable** (or **⋯ Tools**, then **Make the whole book searchable**).
2. Sidepage reads every page. The bar shows **Reading pages 12 / 80 · stop**; you can keep reading meanwhile, or click it to stop.

When it is done, the book can be searched, read aloud, summed up in the [study guide](/study/guide) and quizzed on, like any other book. This is done once per book.

## Put the words of one area into your notes

1. Click **⋯ Tools**, then **Read the text in a box**.
2. Drag a box over the area with the words (a paragraph, a table, a diagram's labels).
3. The words are added to your notes.

## Put the words of a whole page into your notes

1. Go to the page.
2. Click **⋯ Tools**, then **Read the text of this whole page**.

## Pictures

When you open a picture (a photo of the board, a screenshot of a slide), click **Read text** in its bar: every word in the picture goes into your notes.

## Tips for good results

- A sharp, straight, well lit page reads best.
- Zoom in on small print before drawing the box.
- Check names and numbers afterwards: they are what OCR most often gets wrong.
