# Open books and files

Sidepage opens almost any kind of book or document: PDF, EPUB and other e-books, Word, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, comics, DjVu, text, Markdown, web pages and pictures. The full list is in [File formats](/reference/formats).

## Open a file

**From Home:**

1. Click **Open a book**.
2. Pick the file and click **Open**.

**From anywhere:**

- Click **Open** in the top bar, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd> on a Mac).
- Or drag the file from your desktop or a folder onto the Sidepage window.

The file opens with its notes beside it. If you have opened it before, your notes, highlights and bookmarks are all there, and the book opens where you left it.

## Open a file from outside Sidepage

On Windows, Mac and Linux, Sidepage is offered for all the files it can read:

- **Right-click a file**, choose **Open with**, then **Sidepage**.
- **Double-click** a file, once Sidepage is the app for that kind of file. See [Make Sidepage your PDF app](/data/default-app).

If Sidepage is already open, the file opens in the same window, as a new tab.

**On Android:** in your **Files** app (or in a message, an email or a browser download), tap the file, then choose **Sidepage**. Or tap **Share** and pick **Sidepage**.

## Open a book you opened before

Every book you open goes into **Your library** on Home.

1. Go to **Home** (click the Sidepage logo at the top left).
2. Under **Continue reading**, click **Pick up where you left off** for the book you read last.
3. For any other book, scroll to **Your library** and click it.

To find a book fast, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and type part of its name.

### Keep the library tidy

- **Search:** type in the search box above the library (it appears once you have more than six books).
- **Order:** choose **Recently opened** or another order in the box at the right.
- **Ready made shelves:** the buttons above the library show **All**, **Favourites**, **Reading**, **Finished**, and one for each kind of file (PDFs, books, slides…). Click one to see only those books.
- **Star a book** to put it under **Favourites**: hover over it and click the star.
- **Shelves:** to group books (a course, a project), hover over a book and click **Put on a shelf of your own** (the shelf icon), then tick a shelf or type the name of a new one. Your shelves appear as buttons above the library; click one to see only its books. With a shelf chosen, you can **Rename the shelf** or **Take the shelf down** (the books stay in your library).
- **Plan** a book: hover over it and click the calendar icon to set a day to finish it by. See [Reading plans](/study/plans).
- **Remove a book** from the library: hover over it and click **×** (Remove from the library). Its notes are kept; open the book again and they come back.

## Open several books at once

Open books one after another: each one is added as a **tab** above the book. Click a tab to switch books; the notes beside it switch too.

To see two books at once, see [Two books side by side](/reading/side-by-side).

## Close a book

Click the **×** on the book's tab. Your place, notes and highlights are kept.
