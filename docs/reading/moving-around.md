# Move around a book

## Turn pages

- Click **‹** or **›** in the book's bar.
- Or scroll. PDFs scroll from page to page; e-books turn page by page.
- On a keyboard, use the arrow keys, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd>, or the space bar in e-books.
- On a phone or tablet, swipe.

## Go to a page

1. Click the page number in the book's bar (**3 / 21**).
2. Type the page number and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

After a jump, a **↩ p. 3** button appears in the bar. Click it to go back to the page you came from.

## Use the contents

1. Click **Contents** in the book's bar (the list icon). It shows when the book has chapters.
2. Click a chapter.

## See every page at once (PDF)

1. Click **⋯ Tools** in the book's bar.
2. Click **All pages**.
3. Click a page to go to it. Click **All pages** again to close the overview.

## The page map (PDF)

The thin bar at the right edge of a PDF is a map of the whole document:

- Colour marks show **highlights**; a mark on the right half shows a page you **quoted in your notes**; a bookmark mark shows **bookmarks**; a line shows **today's reading goal**.
- **Click anywhere on it, or drag along it**, to go to that part of the document.
- Hover over a mark to see what it is.

## Search in the book

1. Click **Search** in the book's bar.
2. Type a word or phrase and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
3. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> again for the next match. The count shows how many there are.

To search all your books and notes at once, click **🔍** in the top bar instead.

::: tip Scanned PDF finds nothing?
A scanned PDF is pictures of pages, with no words to search. See [Scanned pages and pictures](/reading/scanned-pages) to make it searchable.
:::

## Pick up where you left off

Sidepage remembers the page of every book. Open the book again, from Home or your folders, and it opens there.
