# Read aloud and voices

Sidepage can read a book, or your notes, aloud. The sentence being read is lit on the page so you can follow along. A page in French is read in French, an English page in English, without you choosing.

## Have the book read to you

1. Go to the page where you want to start.
2. Click **⋯ Tools** in the book's bar, then **Read aloud**.
3. Sidepage reads from that page on, turning the pages as it goes.

To stop, click **■** (Stop reading) in the book's bar.

Running heads, page numbers and copyright lines repeated on every page are skipped.

## Have your notes read to you

1. In the note's bar, click **⋯**.
2. Click **Read this note aloud**.

## Choose the voice

1. Open **More** (⋯ at the top right).
2. Under **Reading aloud**, choose **Device voice** or **Natural voice**.

| | Device voice | Natural voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sounds | Depends on your device | Lifelike, on every device |
| Needs | Voices installed on your device | A one time download, about 60 MB per voice |
| Works offline | Yes | Yes, after the download |
| On Android | Often no voices at all | Yes |

**Natural voices:** choose an **English** and a **French** voice from the lists. A ✓ marks the voices already on your device. The first time a voice reads, it downloads (a message shows its progress), then it works without internet.

**Device voices:** pick a voice under **Voice**, or leave **Chosen for each page's language**.

## Faster, slower, deeper

In **More**, under **Reading aloud**:

- **Pace:** Slow, Normal, Brisk, Fast or Very fast.
- **Pitch** (device voices): Deep, Lower, Normal, Higher or High.

## Fall asleep to a book

In **More**, under **Reading aloud**, set **Stop after** to 10, 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Reading stops by itself.

## Read aloud is missing

- **On Android with device voices:** many Android devices have no voices that apps can use. Choose **Natural voice** in **More**.
- **Natural voice says it could not be fetched:** the first download needs the internet. Connect and try again.
