# Look up and translate

## Look up a word

1. Select a word in the book (double-click it, or press and hold on a phone).
2. In the bar that comes up beside the word, click **Look up**.

The **Dictionary** card shows the meanings, with examples. Under **In your books**, it shows where the word comes up in the books you have opened, which is often the best explanation of how your course uses it.

Click **Add to my notes** to put the definition in your notes.

Definitions come from the Free Dictionary and Wiktionary, so looking up needs the internet.

## Translate a passage

1. Select a passage in the book.
2. In the bar that comes up beside the words, click **Translate**.
3. Pick the language in **Into**.

Click **Add to my notes** to put the translation in your notes. Sidepage remembers the language you chose.

The passage is sent to MyMemory, a free translation service, so translating needs the internet. Do not translate anything confidential. See [What goes online](/reference/privacy).

## In e-books and Word documents

In PDFs and e-books they are in the bar that comes up beside the selected words; in a Word document, directly on the book's bar.
