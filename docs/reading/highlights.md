# Highlights and bookmarks

## Highlight a passage

1. Select the passage in the book (drag with the mouse; on a phone, press and hold, then drag the handles).
2. Click **Highlight** in the book's bar.
3. Pick a colour: yellow, green, blue or pink.

Use the colours your way, for example yellow for key ideas and pink for things to ask about. Highlights work in PDFs, e-books, Word documents, text and Markdown files.

## Add a comment to a highlight

1. Click **Highlight** in the book's bar. The menu lists every highlight in this book.
2. Under the highlight, type in **Add a comment**.

A highlight with a comment becomes a flashcard: the passage on the front, your comment on the back. See [Flashcards](/study/flashcards).

## Change, find or remove a highlight

Click **Highlight** in the book's bar. For each highlight in the list:

- **Go to it:** click its text.
- **Change its colour:** click another colour under it.
- **Remove it:** click **×** beside it.

## Every highlight from every book

1. Click **Study** in the top bar, then **All highlights**.
2. Pick a colour at the top to see only those, or type in **Search your highlights**.
3. Click a highlight to open the book at that place.

To keep them as a file, click **Save these as a file**: you get a Markdown file with every highlight, grouped by book.

## Bookmark a page

1. Click **Bookmarks** in the book's bar (the ribbon icon).
2. Click **Bookmark this page**.

The ribbon fills in on bookmarked pages. To go to a bookmark, click **Bookmarks** and click it in the list. To remove one, click **×** beside it, or go to the page and click **Remove this bookmark**.
