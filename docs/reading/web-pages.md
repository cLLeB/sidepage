# Read a web page

Open an article from the web like a book: only the article is kept (no menus, adverts or pop ups), and your notes, highlights and quotes work as with any book.

1. On **Home**, click **⋯** (More ways to start), then **Read a web page**. Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Read a web page…**.
2. Paste the address of the article, such as `https://…`.
3. Click **Read it**.

The article opens as a book named after its title, with its author and address at the top. It stays in your library like any other book.

::: warning In the browser version
Most websites do not let one web page read another, so in the browser version this works only for a few sites. The installed app on Windows, Mac, Linux and Android reads any site.
:::

## It says no article could be found

The page is probably not an article: a home page, a search page, or a page that needs you to sign in. Open the article itself and copy its address from there.
