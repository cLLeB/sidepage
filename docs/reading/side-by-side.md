# Two books side by side

Read a lecture and its summary, a paper and its draft, or a text and its translation next to each other, in one window.

## Open a second book beside the first

1. Open the first book.
2. At the end of the book's tab strip, click **⫴** (Open another book beside this one).
3. Pick the second book.

It opens in a column of its own to the right, with its own bar. The notes stay narrower on the right.

Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Open a book beside this one…**.

## Put a book already open beside the other

When two books are open as tabs in the same place:

1. Click the tab of the book you want to move.
2. Click **⫴** (Put this book in its own column) at the end of the tab strip.

You can also drag a book's tab to the right edge of the window.

## Read side by side, full screen

For reading only: no notes, no bars, the two books sharing the whole screen, page edge to page edge.

1. With two books side by side, click **📖** (Read side by side) at the end of either book's tab strip.
   Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Read side by side, full screen**.
2. Read. Each book scrolls on its own; a thin line at the right edge shows where you are in the right hand book.
3. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to come back. Your notes and bars return as they were.

## Fill the window with one side

Click **⤢** at the end of a book's tab strip: that book fills the window. Then press **Focus** (⤢ in the top bar) to give it the whole screen too. Click **⤡** to bring the other side back.

## Compare the two

To see what changed between the two (a draft and a final version), see [Compare two books](/study/compare).

## On a phone

Phones are too narrow for two columns, so these buttons do not show there. Open both books and switch between their tabs.
