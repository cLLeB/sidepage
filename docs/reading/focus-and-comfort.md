# Focus and reading comfort

## Focus: just the page and your notes

Hide every bar and use the whole screen.

- Click **⤢** (Focus) in the top bar, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>.</kbd>.
- To leave, press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or click **⤡** in the top right corner.

In Focus, a small clock sits in the corner, since the device's own clock is hidden.

To read two books in Focus, see [Read side by side, full screen](/reading/side-by-side#read-side-by-side-full-screen).

## Easier on the eyes

Open **More** (⋯ at the top right). Under **Look**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Warmth** | Tints the screen orange, like a night light. Slide to the right for warmer |
| **Dim** | Darkens the screen beyond your device's lowest brightness, for reading in bed |
| **Reading ruler** | A band of light that follows your pointer and dims the rest, to keep your place line by line |
| **Keep the screen on** | Stops the screen from turning off while a book is open |

For a dark page, choose a dark **Theme**. See [Language, theme and paper](/settings/look).

## Background sound while you study

The [study timer](/study/timer) can play rain, the sea, a fire or brown noise while you work.

## After you close a book

When you close a book after reading a while, a small card sums up the sitting: how long you read and how far you got.
