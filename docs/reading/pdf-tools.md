# More tools for PDFs and paper

## Read a PDF as flowing text

On a phone, a PDF's page is often too small to read without zooming and panning. Click **⋯ Tools**, then **Flowing text**: the words of the pages are set at the width of the screen, in the text size and typeface you chose for books (**Aa** in the bar), page after page. Headings stay headings; the lines repeated on every page (a running head, a page number) are left out. The page you reach is kept for when you go back with **Back to the pages**.

It needs the PDF's text: a scanned PDF must be [made searchable](/reading/scanned-pages) first.

## Selected words

Select words on a PDF page: a bar comes up beside them. Click **⋯** in it for these:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Plot this formula** | Draws the formula as a graph, with a slider for each constant (in `a*x^2 + b`, sliders for **a** and **b**). Fix the formula in the box if the PDF's text came out oddly. **Put the graph in my notes** keeps it |
| **Convert units** | A measurement such as **25 psi**, **500 µm** or **120 km/h** in the other units of its kind |
| **Gap cards from this passage** | Each sentence of the passage with one of the book's terms hidden; untick the ones you do not want and click **Make cards**. They come up in [Review](/study/flashcards) |

## A table into your notes

1. Click **⋯ Tools**, then **A table**.
2. Drag a box round the whole table on the page.

The table goes into your notes as a table you can edit, its rows and columns as on the page.

## A new PDF from some pages

Click **⋯ Tools**, then **New PDF**:

- Under **Pages**, write the pages to keep, such as **3-7, 10**, then click **Save these pages as a PDF**.
- Or click **Join another PDF after it** and choose a PDF: one PDF with both is saved.

The book itself stays as it is.

## Text from a photo of a paper page

For a paper book, a handout or a board:

1. In your notes, click **+** (Insert), then **Text from a photo of a page**.
2. Click **Take or choose a photo**. On a phone or tablet, the camera opens.
3. Click **Every word of the page**, or **Only what I highlighted** to keep just the passages you ran a highlighter over (yellow, green, pink, orange or blue), each as a quote.

The words are read on your device. Take the photo straight on and in good light.
