# Privacy policy

*Applies to Sidepage on every platform: Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and the web app. Effective 30 October 2026.*

Sidepage is a reading and note taking app made by Caleb Kwabena Kyere Boateng ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

- Sidepage has **no account**, **no server** of its own, **no advertising** and **no analytics or tracking**.
- Your books, notes, highlights, drawings, recordings and flashcards **stay on your device**.
- A few features you choose to use send the text you give them to an outside service, as described below. Nothing is sent unless you use them.
- We never sell, rent or share any information about you, and we never see your files.

## What stays on your device

Everything you open or create in Sidepage is stored only on your device (or, on a computer, in a folder you can see: Documents/Sidepage): the books and documents you open, your notes, highlights, bookmarks, canvases, mind maps, flashcards and their schedule, reading progress and settings, and sound you record.

Sidepage processes the following **on your device only**, without sending them anywhere:

- **Microphone recordings** made with "Record sound". The microphone is used only while you record, only after you allow it, and the recording is stored with your notes. Turning speech into text ("Write it out") runs on your device.
- **Text recognition** in scanned pages and pictures.
- **Search by meaning** in your books.
- **Reading aloud**.

## Features that send information to outside services

When you use one of the features below, Sidepage sends the information listed to the service named, so that the service can answer. Each service handles that information under its own privacy policy. These features are optional; the rest of Sidepage works without them.

| Feature | Service | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look up a word | Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) and Wiktionary (Wikimedia Foundation) | The word you look up |
| Translate | MyMemory (Translated srl) | The passage you translate |
| Check spelling and grammar | LanguageTool (LanguageTooler GmbH) | The text of the note you check |
| Papers | OpenAlex (OurResearch) | The title, DOI or words you search for |
| Free PDF, Read a web page | The website or publisher concerned | A request for the page or file you asked for |
| Ask AI (computers only, not Android) | Anthropic, OpenAI or Google, through their own apps, under your own account with them | Your question and the parts of the book and notes the AI reads to answer |

In every case, standard technical information that any internet request carries (such as your IP address) reaches the service.

## Downloads

The first time you use natural voices, speech to text, search by meaning or text recognition, Sidepage downloads the needed voice or model from Hugging Face or jsDelivr. These downloads send no information about you or your files. The versions of Sidepage installed from GitHub check GitHub for updates; the version installed from Google Play is updated by Google Play.

## Permissions on Android

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Internet | For the optional online features above and the one time downloads |
| Microphone (Record audio) | Only to record sound beside your notes, when you choose to |

## Children

Sidepage is a general reading and study app. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and has no account or social features.

## Your choices

- Do not use the online features for text you consider confidential.
- Delete your notes in Sidepage, or uninstall Sidepage, to remove everything it stored. On a computer you can also delete the Documents/Sidepage folder.
- Deny or withdraw the microphone permission at any time in your device's settings.
