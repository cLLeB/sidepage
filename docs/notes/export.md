# Save, export and print

Your notes are always saved by themselves. This page is about taking them out of Sidepage: as a Word document, a web page, a printout or a file for another app.

## One note

Click **⋯** in the note's bar, then:

| Choose | You get |
| --- | --- |
| **Save as a Word document** | A real `.docx` file, with your headings, lists, tables, colours and pictures, that opens in Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice |
| **Save as a web page** | An `.html` file that opens in any browser |
| **Print this note** | Your printer, or **Save as PDF** in the print window (computer only) |
| **Copy the whole note** | The note's text, ready to paste anywhere |

## Everything about one book

1. Open the book.
2. Open **More** (⋯ at the top right).
3. Choose:
   - **Export notes**: the book's notes, highlights and bookmarks as one Markdown file
   - **Make a study pack**: notes, highlights, glossary and questions for the book, as one document to read or print
   - **Save as one file**: the book together with all its notes, as a `.sidepage` file. Open it in Sidepage on any device to get the book and its notes back. Good for sending a book with your notes to a friend.

## Other things you can save

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| A canvas page as a picture | Canvas, **Save as a picture** |
| A mind map as nested points | Mind map, **Save as an outline** |
| Every highlight from every book | Study, All highlights, **Save these as a file** |
| Your flashcards for Anki | Study, Review flashcards, **Save the cards for Anki** |
| A bibliography of your books | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, **Save a bibliography of every book (BibTeX)** or **Save a reading list of every book (APA)** |
| A PDF with your highlights and drawings | In the PDF, **⋯ Tools**, **Save a copy with my marks** |
| Everything, to keep safe | More, **Back up all notes**. See [Back up, restore and move](/data/backup) |

## Where saved files go

On a computer, Sidepage asks where to save each file. On a phone or tablet, files go to your **Downloads** folder.
