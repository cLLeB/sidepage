# Spelling, find and earlier versions

## Check spelling and grammar

1. Click **⋯** in the note's bar, then **Check spelling and grammar**.
2. Mistakes are listed with suggestions. Click a suggestion to apply it.

The note's language is recognised by itself (English, French and about thirty others). The check is done by LanguageTool, an online service: your note's text is sent to it. Do not use it on anything confidential. See [What goes online](/reference/privacy).

## Find and replace

1. Click **⋯** in the note's bar, then **Find and replace**.
2. Type in **Find in this note**. Matches are lit in the note.
3. Type in **Replace with**, then replace one match or all of them.

To search every note and every book at once, click **🔍** in the top bar.

## Earlier versions of a note

Sidepage keeps earlier versions of each note as you write, about every ten minutes.

1. Click **⋯** in the note's bar, then **Earlier versions**.
2. Pick a version on the left. The panel shows what changed since then: lines added in green, lines taken out in red.
3. To go back to that version, click **Bring this version back**.

## Count words

The number of words in the note is shown in the note's bar, after **Saved**. When the note has a checklist, the bar also shows how many boxes are ticked (**3 of 5 done**).
