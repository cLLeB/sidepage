# Canvas and mind map

Besides written notes, every book has a **canvas** to draw and handwrite on, and a **mind map**. Open them from the **⌄** beside **Notes** in the top bar.

## Canvas: draw and handwrite

1. Click **⌄** beside **Notes**, then **Canvas**. Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>2</kbd>.
2. Pick a tool from the canvas toolbar: pen, shapes, arrows, text, eraser.
3. Draw.

The canvas uses Excalidraw, so you can also move, resize and group what you drew, and change colours and line styles.

**With a stylus or Apple Pencil:** touching the page with the pen starts drawing straight away.

**Clips:** with the canvas in front, [clipping](/notes/quotes-and-clips#clip-a-picture-from-the-page) a part of the book puts it on the canvas, where you can annotate it.

### Pages of drawing

The canvas is a notebook of pages:

- **Next page** and **Previous page** move between pages.
- **Add a page at the end** adds a new one.
- **Flip** (Leaf through the notebook) turns the pages like paper.

### Save a drawing

Click **Save as a picture** to save the page you are on as a PNG picture.

## Mind map

1. Click **⌄** beside **Notes**, then **Mind map**. Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>3</kbd>.
2. The book's title is in the middle. Select a topic and press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to add a branch under it, or <kbd>Enter</kbd> to add one beside it.
3. Double-click a topic to rename it. Drag topics to move them.

### Grow the map for you

- **Grow from my notes:** adds the headings and bullet points of your written notes as branches.
- **Grow from the book's ideas:** adds the book's key terms as branches, each gathered under the one it goes with.

Both add to the map; they never replace what you made.

### Save the map

**Save as an outline** saves the map as a Markdown file of nested points, which you can paste into any document.
