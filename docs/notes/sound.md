# Record sound and write it out

Record a lecture (or yourself explaining something) beside your notes. Every line you write while recording is stamped with its moment, so you can hear what was said when you wrote it. Then turn the speech into text.

## Record

1. Open the notes for the book.
2. Click **⋯** in the note's bar, then **Record sound with this note**.
3. The first time, allow Sidepage to use the microphone.
4. Write your notes as usual. Each new line gets a small **▸ 12:30** stamp: the moment in the recording.
5. To stop, click **⋯**, then **Stop recording sound**.

The recording is kept with the book's notes.

## Listen from a moment

Click a **▸** stamp in your notes. A player opens under the note's bar and plays from that moment.

## Turn the speech into text

1. Click a stamp to open the player for that recording.
2. In the player, choose the language spoken, or leave **Language of the note**.
3. Click **Write it out**.

The speech is written into your notes, passage by passage, each starting with its stamp. It works in English, French and many other languages.

This runs on your device: the recording is never sent anywhere. The first time, Sidepage fetches its speech recogniser (about 80 MB, shown as **Fetching the speech recogniser… 40%**); after that it works offline. A long lecture takes a few minutes to write out.

## Tips

- Put the device near the speaker. A quiet room gives much better text.
- To fix a wrong word in the text, just edit it: it is ordinary text in your notes.
