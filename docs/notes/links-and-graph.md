# Links between notes and the graph

Notes on different books often talk about the same ideas. Link them, and Sidepage shows you how your reading connects.

## Link to another book's notes

1. In your note, type `[[`.
2. A list of your books appears. Type a few letters to narrow it, use the arrow keys and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or click a book.

Or click **+** (Insert) in the note's bar and pick a book under **Link to another book's notes**.

Click the link to open that book with its notes.

## See what links here

When other notes link to this one, the note's bar shows **Linked from** followed by those books. Click one to open it.

## The graph of your notes

1. Click **Study** in the top bar, then **Graph of your notes**.
2. Each dot is a book's notes; each line is a link between them.
3. Click a dot to open that book.

Drag dots to arrange the graph, and scroll or pinch to zoom.
