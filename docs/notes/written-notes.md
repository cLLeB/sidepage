# Write notes

Every book has its own written note, on paper like an exercise book. It opens beside the book and is saved as you type.

## Open the notes

- Click **Notes** in the top bar, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd>.
- The notes always belong to the book in front of you. Switch books, and the notes switch too.
- For notes that belong to no book, use **Quick notes**: on Home click **⋯**, then **Quick notes**.

Your note is saved as you type. The note's bar shows **Saving…** for a moment, then **Saved**. Closing Sidepage saves anything not yet written.

## Headings, lists and checklists

1. Put the cursor in the line.
2. Click **¶** (Kind of line) in the note's bar.
3. Choose **Plain text**, **Large heading**, **Heading**, **Small heading**, **Bullet list**, **Numbered list**, **Checklist** or **Quote**.

Shortcuts while typing, at the start of a line:

| Type | You get |
| --- | --- |
| `# ` `## ` `### ` | Large heading, heading, small heading |
| `- ` or `* ` | Bullet list |
| `1. ` | Numbered list |
| `- [ ] ` | Checklist |
| `> ` | Quote |

**Move, copy or remove a whole line:** in the same **¶** menu, under **This line**, use the arrows to move it up or down, the copy button to make a copy under it, or the bin to remove it.

## Bold, colours and marker

1. Select the words.
2. In the note's bar, click:
   - **B**, **I** or **U** for bold, italic or underline
   - **A** (Text colour) and pick a colour, or **The page's own ink** to remove it
   - **Marker** and pick a colour, or **No marker**

Words run over with the marker become a flashcard, with those words hidden. See [Flashcards](/study/flashcards).

## Size, typeface, alignment

1. Select the words, or put the cursor in the paragraph for alignment.
2. Click **Aa** (Format) in the note's bar.
3. Choose:
   - **Strike through**, **Raised** or **Lowered** (for x² or H₂O)
   - a **Size**: Normal, Small, Large or Huge
   - a **Typeface**: Plain, Book, Handwriting or Typewriter
   - an **Alignment**: Left, Centre, Right or Fill the line
   - **Plain writing** to take every look off the selected words

## The paper and your handwriting

To change the whole note's look:

1. Click **⋯** in the note's bar.
2. Under **Paper and writing**, choose:
   - **Typeface** and **Size** of your writing
   - **Ink**: Pencil, Blue ink, Black ink, Green ink or Red ink
   - **Lines**: Narrow, Normal or Wide
   - **Paper** colour: White, Cream, Legal yellow, Pale blue, Pale green or Pale pink

The lines of the paper follow the size you choose. To change the kind of paper (blank, lined, grid or dots) for every note, see [Language, theme and paper](/settings/look).

## Undo and redo

- **Undo:** **↶** in the note's bar, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd>.
- **Redo:** **⋯**, then **Redo**, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Y</kbd>.

## Keep the line you write at the same height

**⋯**, then **Typewriter writing**. The line you are writing stays in the middle of the screen and the page moves up instead.

## Lecture notes: a heading for each slide

When you take notes on slides or a PDF lecture:

1. Click **⋯** in the note's bar.
2. Click **A heading for each page or slide**.

Now when you turn to a slide, your note jumps to that slide's heading, creating it the first time you write there. Your notes end up in slide order without any effort.
