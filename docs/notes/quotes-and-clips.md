# Quotes and clips from the book

Take words or pictures from the book into your notes without typing them again.

## Quote a passage

1. Select the passage in the book.
2. Click **Quote** in the book's bar.

The passage is added at the end of your notes, as the next line in a notebook. It goes in as plain text, ready for you to underline, colour or comment on.

## Clip a picture from the page

For a diagram, a formula, a table or a figure:

1. Click **⋯ Tools** in the book's bar, then **Clip a picture into my notes**.
2. Drag a box over the part of the page you want.
3. The picture goes into your notes.

To stop clipping, click the clip button that stays on the bar.

When your **Canvas** is open in front, the clip goes onto the canvas instead, where you can draw on it. See [Canvas and mind map](/notes/canvas-and-mind-map).

## Turn a diagram into flashcards (picture cards)

For a labelled diagram (a cell, a circuit, a map):

1. Click **⋯ Tools**, then **Picture cards**.
2. Drag a box over the diagram.
3. In the window that opens, drag a box over each label you want to learn. Optionally type **What is under it**, shown as the answer.
4. Click **Make picture cards**.

Each box becomes a flashcard: the diagram with that label covered, then uncovered. See [Flashcards and review](/study/flashcards).

## Words from a scanned page

For a page that is a picture of words, use **⋯ Tools**, then **Read the text in a box**. See [Scanned pages and pictures](/reading/scanned-pages).

## Keep pace with the book

Turn the pages, and your notes scroll to what you quoted or clipped from each page.

1. Click **⋯** in the note's bar.
2. Click **Keep pace with the book**.
