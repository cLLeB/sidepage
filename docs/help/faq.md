# Questions and answers

## About Sidepage

### Is Sidepage free?

Yes, completely, with no account, adverts or paid version. It is open source (GPL-3.0).

### Does Sidepage need the internet?

No. Reading, notes, flashcards, the study guide and everything else work offline. A few features go online when you use them (look up, translate, spelling, papers, Ask AI); some download a model the first time. See [What goes online](/reference/privacy).

### Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Only [Ask AI](/ai/ask-ai) uses an account: your own AI subscription.

### Which devices does it run on?

Windows, Mac, Linux and Android as an installed app; iPhone, iPad, Chromebook and any browser as a web app. See [Install Sidepage](/get-started/install).

### Which version do I have?

Open **More** (⋯ at the top right): the version is at the bottom.

## Notes

### Where are my notes?

On your device. On a computer, in **Documents/Sidepage**, one folder per book. See [Where your notes are kept](/data/where-notes-live).

### Do my notes sync between my laptop and my tablet?

Not by themselves. To use the same notes on two devices, [back up on one and restore on the other](/data/backup#move-to-a-new-computer-phone-or-tablet), or keep **Documents/Sidepage** in a synced folder (OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox) on your computers.

### Can I open my notes in other apps?

Yes. Written notes are Markdown files (`notes.md`) that any text editor opens. You can also [save a note as a Word document](/notes/export).

### I renamed a PDF and my notes are gone

Notes belong to a book by its file name. Rename the file back, open it, and your notes are there.

### Can two books share one set of notes?

A PDF and an EPUB with the same name share their notes. Otherwise, [link the notes](/notes/links-and-graph) of the two books to each other.

## Reading

### Can Sidepage open Kindle books?

Kindle books without DRM (`.mobi`, `.azw3` from other sources) yes. Books bought from Amazon are locked to Amazon's apps.

### Can I write on the PDF itself?

Yes: in a PDF, **⋯ Tools**, then **Draw**. Your drawings are kept and shown every time you open the PDF. **⋯ Tools**, **Save a copy with my marks** gives you a PDF with your highlights and drawings in it.

### Can it read my book aloud in French?

Yes: French pages are read in French by themselves. For a lifelike voice, choose **Natural voice** in **More**. See [Read aloud](/reading/read-aloud).

## Study and AI

### What is the difference between the Guide and Ask AI?

The [Guide](/study/guide) works on every device, offline, instantly and free, from the book's own words: gist, key terms, quiz, glossary, timeline. [Ask AI](/ai/ask-ai) converses: it explains, reasons and rephrases, but needs your AI subscription and a computer.

### Can I use Ask AI without a subscription?

Gemini CLI can be used with a free Google account, within Google's free limits. Claude and ChatGPT need their paid plans.

### Can I use Ask AI on my phone?

No. Ask AI runs the AI's own app, which exists only for computers. On a phone, use the [Guide](/study/guide) and [Ask your books](/study/ask-your-books).

### Can I move my flashcards to Anki?

Yes: **Study**, **Review flashcards**, **Save the cards for Anki**. See [Flashcards](/study/flashcards#take-your-cards-to-anki).

## Language

### Can Sidepage be in French?

Yes: **More**, **Language**, **Français**. See [Language, theme and paper](/settings/look).
