# Language practice

Learn a language from the books you read in it. The **Language practice** panel works on the page you are on: it shows the words you do not know yet, gathers the ones worth learning into flashcards, puts a translation under each sentence, and gives you the page's sentences for dictation and for saying back.

## Open it

1. Open a book in the language you are learning, and go to a page.
2. Click **Study**, then **Language practice**.

The panel opens beside the book and follows the page you are on. At the top, under **Meanings in**, choose the language you know: meanings and translations come in it.

A long page or chapter is taken a part at a time: **Earlier sentences** and **Later sentences** move between parts.

## Read: see the words you do not know

The page is shown with each word you have not marked as known lit in blue. Words you are learning are lit in amber.

- Tap a word for its meaning, then **Learn it** (it becomes a flashcard with its sentence) or **I know it**.
- When the blue words left are all ones you know, tap **I know the rest**.

At the top, **You know 87% of the words here** shows how far you have come. Words you mark are known in every book in that language.

## Words: the words worth learning

The words of the page you have not marked as known, the ones the book uses most first (learning them pays off on every page after). Each comes with its sentence.

- **Meaning** shows what it means.
- **+** learns it: a flashcard with its sentence on the front and its meaning on the back.
- **✓** marks it as known.

Under them, **Words you are learning from this book** lists the words you saved. Their cards come up in [Review](/study/flashcards).

## Both languages

Each sentence of the page with its translation under it. The sentences are sent one at a time to MyMemory, a free translation service, which has a daily limit; when it is reached, Sidepage says so.

## Dictation

1. Click **Listen**: a sentence of the page is read aloud. Tick **Slower** to hear it slowly.
2. Write what you hear, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click **Check**.

The words you missed are underlined in red and words that are not in the sentence are struck through, with the sentence shown under them. **Next sentence** goes on.

## Say it back

1. Click **Listen**.
2. Click **Say it** and say the sentence the same way, then click **Stop**.

Sidepage writes out what you said, on your device, and shows how much was right and which words it did not hear. **Hear yourself** plays your recording. The first time, the speech recogniser is fetched (about 80 MB); after that it works offline, and your voice never leaves the device.

## Your words travel with you

The words you know are part of [backups](/data/backup). The words you are learning from a book are kept with its notes.
