# Study together

A room for your study group, each on their own phone, tablet or computer: see where the others are reading, follow one of them page by page, send a few words, and play a quiz duel on a page.

## Start or join a room

1. Click **Study**, then **Study together**.
2. Write **Your name**, so the others know who you are.
3. Click **Start a room**. A code shows, such as **abcde-fghij**: click **Copy the code** and give it to the others.
4. The others open **Study together**, write their name, paste the code and click **Join**.

Under **Here with you**, each person shows with the book and page they are reading.

## Follow someone

Click **Follow** beside a name: as they turn their pages, yours turn with them, in the same book (when it is on your device too). Click **Following** to stop.

## Quiz duel

1. Go to the page to be tested on.
2. Click **Start a duel on this page**.

Everyone in the room gets the same five questions from that page and the next ones, made from the book's own terms. The most right answers wins; the quickest breaks a tie. The scores show as people answer.

## Messages

Write a few words under **Messages** and click **Send**: everyone in the room sees them.

## Privacy

The devices find each other through public relays, then talk straight to one another, encrypted with the room's code. Nothing goes through a server of Sidepage's, and nothing is kept once you click **Leave**. Only people with the code can join.
