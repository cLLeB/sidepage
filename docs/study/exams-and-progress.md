# Mock exams and progress

## Take a mock exam

A timed test of four-answer questions, made from your [flashcards](/study/flashcards).

1. Click **Study** in the top bar, then **Review flashcards**.
2. Click **Mock exam**.
3. Tick the books it should cover.
4. Choose how many **Questions** (10, 20, 30 or 50) and the **Time** (5 to 60 minutes, or **No limit**).
5. Click **Begin**.
6. For each question, click an answer, then **Next**. Click **Finish now** to stop early.

At the end you see your score, and under **To look at again** the questions you got wrong, each with the right answer. Click **Another exam** to go again.

A mock exam needs at least four cards with answers.

## See how your learning is going

In **Review flashcards**, once you are **All caught up**, the panel shows:

- **Known, learning, new**: how many of your cards you know well (you remember them for three weeks or more), are still learning, or have not seen yet.
- **The next two weeks**: how many cards come up each day, so you can plan.
- **How often you remembered**, week by week: how many answers you gave and how many you got right.
