# Reading plans, goals and the calendar

## Finish a book by a date

Set a day to finish a book, and Sidepage tells you each day how far to read.

1. On **Home**, either:
   - under **Continue reading**, click **Set a day to finish it by**, or
   - hover over the book in **Your library** and click the calendar icon.
2. Pick the date.

From then on, **Home** shows the book under **Today's reading**:

- **Finish by Fri 14 Nov · 9 days left**
- **Read to 45% today · 12% to go**, with a bar showing where you are and today's mark

When you reach today's mark, it says **Done for today**. In a PDF, the book's bar also shows **Today: to p. 84**, and the page map at the right edge marks that page.

The plan adjusts itself: read ahead today and tomorrow's share is lighter; miss a day and it is shared over the days left.

**Change the date:** on Home, click **Change the finish day** beside the plan. **Stop the plan:** click **Drop this plan**.

## A daily reading goal

1. Open **More** (⋯ at the top right).
2. Under **Reading**, set **Daily goal**, from 15 minutes up to 2 hours a day.

Home then shows **12 min of 30 min today** in the line under **Continue reading**. Tap that line for the last twelve weeks of reading, day by day.

## Put your plans and reviews in your calendar

Get reminders in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Put reading plans and reviews in your calendar (.ics)**.
2. Save the file.
3. Open it on your computer or phone, or import it in your calendar app:
   - **Google Calendar:** Settings, **Import & export**, **Import**, choose the file.
   - **Outlook:** **Add calendar**, **Upload from file**.
   - **Apple Calendar:** double-click the file.

You get a daily reminder to read each planned book until its finish day, the finish day itself, and a short review session on each of the coming days when flashcards are due.

The calendar file is a snapshot. When your plans change, make a new one.
