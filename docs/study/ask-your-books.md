# Ask your books

Type a question, and Sidepage finds the passages across all your books that answer it best. It works offline and costs nothing; for a conversation that explains and reasons, use [Ask AI](/ai/ask-ai).

## Ask

1. Click **Study** in the top bar, then **Guide**. Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Ask your books a question**.
2. In **Ask your books**, type your question, such as *why do dry electrodes have higher impedance*.
3. Click **Ask**.

The best passages are listed with their book and page, the question's words lit. Click a passage to open the book there.

## Search by meaning

By default Sidepage looks for the words of your question. To find passages that say the same thing in other words, or in another language:

1. Tick **By meaning, not only by the words** under the question box.
2. Ask as usual.

The first time, Sidepage fetches a small language model (about 120 MB) and reads each of your books once to learn what each passage means. That takes a minute or two; after that, asking by meaning is quick and works offline. It understands English, French and many other languages, and finds a French passage for an English question.

## Good questions

- Use the words your course uses: *what is the Péclet number* finds more than *what is that number*.
- Ask about one thing at a time.
- If nothing comes up, try **By meaning**, or other words.

## Only books you have opened

Sidepage learns a book's words the first time you open it. A book that is in your library but was never opened on this device is not searched yet: open it once.
