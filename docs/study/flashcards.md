# Flashcards and review

Sidepage makes flashcards from what you already do while reading, so you never type a card. It then brings each card back just before you would forget it (using FSRS, the scheduling method of modern flashcard apps).

## Where cards come from

| You do this | You get this card |
| --- | --- |
| Highlight a passage and [add a comment](/reading/highlights#add-a-comment-to-a-highlight) | Front: the passage. Back: your comment |
| Highlight a passage with no comment | The passage with its key term hidden: recall the hidden word |
| In your notes, write a `Q:` line with an `A:` line under it | Front: the question. Back: the answer |
| In your notes, run the **Marker** over some words | The line with those words hidden |
| Make [picture cards](/notes/quotes-and-clips#turn-a-diagram-into-flashcards-picture-cards) on a diagram | The diagram with one label covered: recall the label |

For example, in your notes:

```
Q: What is the Péclet number?
A: The ratio of transport by flow to transport by diffusion, Pe = vw/D.
```

## Review your cards

1. Click **Study** in the top bar, then **Review flashcards**.
2. Read the front. Recall the answer.
3. Click **Show the other side** (or press the space bar).
4. Say how it went: **Again** (I did not remember), **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy** (I knew it at once). Or press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd>.

The next card comes up. Cards you found hard come back sooner; easy ones later. When nothing is due, the panel says **All caught up**.

To see where a card came from, click **Open in the book**.

## Review everything now

When nothing is due but you want to go through all your cards anyway (the night before an exam), click **Practise every card anyway**. This does not change when cards are due.

## Take your cards to Anki

1. In **Review flashcards**, click **Save the cards for Anki**.
2. Open the saved `.apkg` file with Anki (computer), AnkiDroid (Android) or AnkiMobile (iPhone).

Each book becomes its own deck; hidden word cards become Anki cloze cards; picture cards keep their pictures. Saving again later updates the cards already in Anki instead of adding them twice.

## No cards?

The panel says **No cards yet** until you have made some in one of the ways above. Make a few, then click **Check again**.
