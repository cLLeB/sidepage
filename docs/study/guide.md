# The study guide

The study guide works out, from the book's own words, what the page you are on is about: its gist, the book's key terms, a quiz, a glossary, a timeline and the places in your other books on the same subject. It is instant, free and works offline, on every device.

The guide follows you: turn the page, and it updates for the new page.

## Open the guide

1. Open a book and go to the page you are studying.
2. Click **Study** in the top bar, then **Guide**.

## This page in brief

The few sentences that carry most of the page's weight, in the order they were written.

Click **Add to my notes** to put them in your notes.

## Test yourself on this page

A short quiz on the page: each question leaves out one of the book's key terms, with four choices. Click your answer; right answers turn green, wrong ones red.

## What this book turns on

The book's key terms, the larger the more the book uses them. Click a term to see:

- the pages where it is thickest, with how often it comes up (**p. 12 · 8×**); click one to go there;
- **Teach it back**: explain the term in your own words without looking, then click **Check my explanation**. Sidepage shows what share of the book's key ideas on it you brought in (**You brought in**), what the book also speaks of that you left out (**The book also speaks of**), and the passage where the book explains it.

Below the terms:

- **Show how its ideas connect** draws a map of the terms, linked when they come up together. Click a term on the map to pick it.
- **Make a glossary of these terms** finds where the book says what each term is. Click an entry to go to that page, or **Put the glossary in my notes**.

## Timeline of this book

For a book that speaks of dates (history, a biography, the story of a discovery): click **Show the … dates it speaks of** for every year it mentions, with the sentence saying what happened. Click a date to go to that page, or **Put the timeline in my notes**.

## Elsewhere in your books

Passages in your other books that speak of the same things as this page. Click one to open that book at that place.

## Ask your books

A question box that searches every book you have opened. See [Ask your books](/study/ask-your-books).

## The guide shows nothing?

- **Scanned PDF:** the guide needs words. [Make the book searchable](/reading/scanned-pages) first.
- **Just opened:** Sidepage gathers a book's words the first time you open it; with a long book this takes a few seconds.
- **For deeper questions** ("why does this work?", "explain this simply"), use [Ask AI](/ai/ask-ai).
