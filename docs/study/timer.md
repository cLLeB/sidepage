# Study timer

Study for a stretch, then take a break (the Pomodoro way), with an optional background sound.

## Start

1. Click **Study** in the top bar, then **Study timer**.
2. Set **Study for** (15 to 60 minutes) and **Then rest for** (3 to 15 minutes).
3. Click **Start … minutes of study**.

While it runs, the time left shows in the top bar (**Study 24:10**). When the stretch ends, Sidepage says **That is 25 minutes of study done. Take 5 minutes off.**, and when the break ends, **Break over. Ready for another stretch?**

To stop early, click the time in the top bar, then **Stop**.

## Sound while you work

In the same timer menu, under **Sound while you work**, choose **Rain**, **The sea**, **A fire** or **Brown noise**, and set the **Volume**. Choose **Silence** to turn it off. The sound plays whether or not the timer is running.
