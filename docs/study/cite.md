# Cite and bibliographies

## Cite the book you are reading

1. In a PDF, click **⋯ Tools** in the book's bar, then **Cite this book…**. In any book, you can also press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Cite this book…**.
2. Pick the **Style**: **APA**, **MLA**, **Chicago** or **BibTeX**.
3. Copy what you need:
   - **In the list of references**: the full reference
   - **In your text**: the short citation, with the page you are on
   - **The selected passage, with its source**: select a passage in the book first, then open Cite
   - **BibTeX entry**: for LaTeX and reference managers

Click a line to copy it; it says **Copied**.

## Fix the details

Sidepage takes the title, authors, year and publisher from the file itself. Files often get these wrong or leave them out (it then says **This file does not name its author**).

1. In the Cite window, click **Edit the details (title, authors, year, publisher)**.
2. Correct the **Title**, the **Authors** (one per line), the **Year** and the **Publisher**.
3. Click **Done with the details**.

Sidepage remembers the details for that book.

## A bibliography of all your books

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose:

- **Save a bibliography of every book (BibTeX)**: a `.bib` file for LaTeX, Zotero or Mendeley
- **Save a reading list of every book (APA)**: a Markdown list
