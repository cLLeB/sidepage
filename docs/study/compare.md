# Compare two books

See what changed between two versions of a document: a draft and the paper it became, last year's lecture and this year's, a condensed summary and the full text.

## Compare

1. Open both documents once (Sidepage needs to have read them).
2. Click **Study** in the top bar, then **Compare two books**.
3. Pick the older one in **Earlier** and the newer one in **Later**. If two books are [side by side](/reading/side-by-side), they are picked for you. Click **⇄** (Swap) if they are the wrong way round.

Sidepage shows:

- a summary: **6305 words added, 1405 taken out, on 2 pages**;
- every change, with the page it is on in the later version: what was taken out in **red** and crossed out, what was added in **green**, with a few words around it.

Click a change to open the later version at that page.

**Ignore capital letters** is on by default, so "Diffusion" and "diffusion" count as the same. Untick it to see changes in capitals too.

## How it compares

Sidepage first matches the two documents sentence by sentence, then compares edited sentences word by word. So it handles small edits and large rewrites alike. A whole new passage shows as added, page by page.

## It says the two are too different

The documents have almost nothing in common, so a comparison would not help. Check that you picked the right two.
