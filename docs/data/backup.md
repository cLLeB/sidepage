# Back up, restore and move

## Back up all your notes

1. Open **More** (⋯ at the top right).
2. Click **Back up all notes**.
3. Save the file (`Sidepage backup 2026-10-29.zip`).

The backup holds everything written beside every book in your library: notes, canvases, mind maps, highlights, bookmarks, pictures and recordings in your notes, picture cards, earlier versions, and your flashcard schedule. It does not hold the books themselves.

Keep the file somewhere other than the device: a USB stick, your email, or cloud storage.

## Restore a backup

1. Open **More**.
2. Click **Restore a backup**.
3. Pick the backup file.

Sidepage says how many files it put back. Nothing already on the device is overwritten: notes you have here are kept, and only what is missing is added. Open a book, and its notes from the backup are there.

## Move to a new computer, phone or tablet

1. On the old device, [back up](#back-up-all-your-notes) and copy the backup file to the new device.
2. Copy your books too (PDFs and others), since the backup does not hold them.
3. On the new device, install Sidepage and [restore the backup](#restore-a-backup).
4. Open each book: its notes appear beside it.

On a computer, you can instead copy the whole **Documents/Sidepage** folder. See [Where your notes are kept](/data/where-notes-live).

## Send one book with its notes

To give someone a book with your notes, or to carry one book to another device:

1. Open the book.
2. Open **More**, then **Save as one file**.
3. Send the `.sidepage` file.

Opening it in Sidepage, on any device, opens the book with all its notes, highlights and drawings.

## Before uninstalling on Android

Uninstalling removes the notes kept inside the app. Back up first, then restore after installing again.
