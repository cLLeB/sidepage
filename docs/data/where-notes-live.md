# Where your notes are kept

Your notes stay on your device. Sidepage has no account and no server: nothing you write is uploaded anywhere (except the few online features listed in [What goes online](/reference/privacy), and only when you use them).

## In the app on Windows, Mac and Linux

Every book has a folder in your **Documents** folder:

```
Documents
└── Sidepage
    ├── Lecture 3 slides           (one folder for each book, named after it)
    │   ├── notes.md               your written notes
    │   ├── canvas.excalidraw      your canvas
    │   ├── mindmap.json           your mind map
    │   ├── highlights.json        your highlights and their comments
    │   ├── bookmarks-pdf.json     your bookmarks
    │   ├── clip-….jpg             pictures clipped into your notes
    │   └── sound-….webm           your recordings
    └── _review                    when each flashcard is due
```

These are ordinary files. You can open `notes.md` in any text editor, copy the folder to a USB stick, or keep the whole `Sidepage` folder in a synced folder such as OneDrive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox.

The books themselves stay where they were; Sidepage keeps its own copy of each book you open, so it can open it again from your library.

## In the browser (computer)

Notes are kept in the browser's own storage, on this computer and in this browser only.

In Chrome or Edge, you can keep them as files in a folder of your choice instead:

1. Open **More** (⋯ at the top right).
2. Click **Choose notes folder** and pick a folder.

After the browser restarts, it may ask once more for leave: click **Reconnect notes folder** in **More**.

## On Android

Notes are kept inside the Sidepage app. Uninstalling Sidepage removes them, so **[back up](/data/backup)** before uninstalling, or before resetting the device.

## On iPhone and iPad (web app)

Notes are kept in Safari's storage for the Sidepage web app. Back up from time to time: Safari may clear the storage of a web app that has not been opened for several weeks.

## Clearing the browser

In the browser version, clearing your browsing data (cookies and site data) for clleb.github.io deletes your notes. [Back up](/data/backup) first.
