# Link your phone or tablet with your computer

Link your phone or tablet with your computer once, and then, whenever Sidepage is open on both:

- **Ask AI on your phone uses the AI on your computer**: Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI, signed in with your Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan. No key, nothing extra to pay.
- **Send a book with all its notes** from one device to the other: it opens there with its notes, highlights and drawings in place.

No account is needed. The two devices talk straight to each other, encrypted with the code only they know.

## Link them (once)

1. On your **computer**, open Sidepage, then **More** (⋯ at the top right), **Link devices**.
2. Click **Start a link on this device**. A code appears, such as `k7m2q-p9xha`.
3. On your **phone or tablet**, open Sidepage, then **More**, **Link devices**.
4. Type the code into **Or type the code shown on your other device**, and tap **Link the devices**.

After a few seconds both show **Linked with your computer** (or phone, or tablet). The devices remember the link: next time, they find each other by themselves as soon as Sidepage is open on both.

Both devices need the internet to find each other the first time each is opened; they do not need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

## Ask your computer's AI from your phone

1. On the computer, set up Ask AI once with [the AI's app](/ai/ask-ai#with-the-ai-s-app-on-a-computer) (Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI) and sign in.
2. Keep Sidepage open on the computer.
3. On the phone, open a book and tap **Ask AI**. The top of the panel says **Claude on your computer** (or the app you use).
4. Ask as usual. The question goes to your computer; the AI there reads the book and your notes and the answer comes back to your phone as it is written.

The book's text goes to your computer the first time you ask about it, so the AI can search it. A picture of the page you are on goes along too, for pages that are scans or pictures.

If Ask AI on the phone shows **Connect an AI** instead, the computer is not linked just now: open Sidepage on it. You can also tap **Link to my computer** there.

## Send a book with its notes

1. Open the book on one device.
2. Open **More**, **Link devices**.
3. Click **Send** *the book* **and its notes to your** *phone*.

It opens on the other device by itself, with all its notes, highlights, bookmarks and drawings. Notes already on the other device for that book are kept; nothing is overwritten.

## Unlink

Open **More**, **Link devices**, and click **Unlink**. Do it on both devices to forget the link everywhere.

## Privacy

The devices find each other through public relays (the Nostr network) that pass on only an encrypted introduction; after that, everything goes straight from one device to the other, encrypted, and never through a server of Sidepage's. Linking uses [Trystero](https://github.com/dmotz/trystero). Anyone without your code cannot join. Do not share the code.
