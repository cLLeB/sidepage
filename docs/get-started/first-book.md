# Your first book and notes

This walk through takes about five minutes. You will open a book, write a note beside it, put a passage from the book into the note, and highlight a sentence. By the end you will know the moves you use every day.

You need any PDF, e-book or Word document. A lecture's slides as a PDF work well.

## 1. Open a book

1. Start Sidepage. You see **Home**, your library.
2. Click **Open a book** (top right of Home).
3. Pick your file and click **Open**.

The book opens on the left and an empty written note opens beside it, on lined paper. Each book has its own notes; Sidepage keeps them for you and opens them again next time.

::: tip Other ways to open a file
Drag a file from your desktop or folder onto the Sidepage window. Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd>. On a phone, tap the **Open** icon (a folder) at the top left.
:::

## 2. Write a note

1. Click on the paper, where it says **Write here…**
2. Type. Your note is saved as you write; the bar above it says **Saved**.
3. To make a heading, click **¶** (Kind of line) in the note's bar and choose **Heading**. To make a list, choose **Bullet list** there, or simply type `- ` at the start of a line.

## 3. Put a passage from the book into your note

1. In the book, select a sentence or a paragraph with the mouse (or press and hold on a phone, then drag the handles).
2. Click **Quote** in the book's bar (the icon with lines and quote marks).

The passage appears in your note, at the end, ready to be commented on.

## 4. Highlight a sentence

1. Select a sentence in the book.
2. Click **Highlight** in the book's bar and pick a colour.

To find all your highlights later, click **Highlight** again: the list shows every highlight in this book, and a click takes you to it.

## 5. Find your way back

Close Sidepage and open it again. **Home** shows the book under **Continue reading**, at the page you left. Click **Pick up where you left off**: the book opens at that page with your notes beside it.
