# What goes online

Sidepage works offline. Your books and notes stay on your device: there is no Sidepage account and no Sidepage server, and nothing is collected about you.

A few features need the internet. They contact the services below **only when you use them**, and send only what the feature needs.

| When you use | Sidepage contacts | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Look up** | Free Dictionary (dictionaryapi.dev) and Wiktionary | The word |
| **Translate** | MyMemory (mymemory.translated.net) | The selected passage |
| **Check spelling and grammar** | LanguageTool (languagetool.org) | The note's text |
| **Papers** | OpenAlex (openalex.org) | The paper's title or DOI, or what you searched |
| **Free PDF** from Papers | The publisher's site | A request for that PDF |
| **Read a web page** | The website you named | A request for that page |
| **Ask AI** | Anthropic, OpenAI or Google, through their own app, under your account | Your question, and the parts of the book and notes the AI reads |
| **Natural voices** (first time only) | Hugging Face and jsDelivr | Nothing about you: the voice is downloaded |
| **Write it out** and **By meaning** (first time only) | Hugging Face | Nothing about you: the model is downloaded |
| **Read the text** in scans (first time only) | jsDelivr | Nothing about you: the reading engine is downloaded |
| **Updates** | GitHub (github.com) | A check for a newer version |

Recordings you **Write out**, words you **Read** from scans, and searches **By meaning** are processed on your device: the recording, the page and your books are never sent anywhere.

## Do not send confidential text

Translate, spelling and grammar, and Ask AI send text to the services above. Do not use them on anything confidential.

## The web app

The web app at clleb.github.io is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs of visits; Sidepage itself keeps nothing.
