# Google Play listing: English (en-US)

## App name (30 characters at most)

Sidepage

## Short description (80 characters at most)

Read PDFs and e-books with your notes beside them. Study smarter, offline.

## Full description (4000 characters at most)

Read on one side, think on the other.

Sidepage opens your books, lecture slides and papers with your notes right beside them, on lined paper like an exercise book. Highlight, quote and clip from the page into your notes, then turn everything you read into flashcards, quizzes and study guides. No account, no adverts, and your notes never leave your device.

READ ALMOST ANYTHING
• PDF, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, DjVu and comics
• Word, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, RTF, text and Markdown
• Photos of the board and screenshots of slides
• Two documents side by side, or full screen with nothing in the way
• Read aloud with lifelike natural voices, in English, French and more
• Look up words and translate passages without leaving the page
• Make scanned PDFs searchable

NOTES BESIDE THE BOOK
• Written notes on lined, grid or dotted paper, saved as you type
• Quote a passage or clip a diagram from the page in one tap
• Draw and handwrite with a stylus on a canvas, or build a mind map
• Record a lecture beside your notes, then turn the speech into text
• Diagrams, tables, formulas and checklists
• A heading for each slide, so lecture notes stay in order

STUDY SMARTER
• A study guide for every page: the gist, key terms, a quiz, a glossary
• Flashcards made from your highlights and notes, scheduled to beat forgetting
• Picture cards: cover the labels of a diagram and learn them
• Timed mock exams from your cards
• Reading plans: finish a book by a date, with today's share
• Ask your books a question, by words or by meaning
• Compare a draft with its final version, word by word
• Find what a paper cites and what cites it, and open free papers
• Save your cards for Anki, your notes as Word, and your plans in your calendar

YOURS, ON YOUR DEVICE
• Works offline
• No account and no tracking
• Folders: open a set of lectures once and move from one to the next
• Back up all your notes in one file, and restore them on any device
• English and French

Sidepage is free and open source. Full help: clleb.github.io/sidepage/docs
